# Faction — Hamas: "The Underground"

## Identity
Cheap, numerous and elusive. Hamas fights from below and from inside the city: tunnels, ambushes, IEDs, rockets and drones. It wins by attrition, surprise and saturation.

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Tunnel mobility and retreats | No armor |
| Stealth and ambush bonuses | Fragile in open terrain |
| Cheap, numerous squads | Low sustained AT firepower |
| Rocket saturation vs Iron Dome | Tunnels are costly on rocky ground |
| IEDs and EFPs bypass Trophy | Hidden assets collapse once detected |

**Visual identity:** improvised gear, mixed camouflage, workshop-built weapons, concrete and rebar.
**Voice:** Gazan Arabic. Terse, close-quarters callouts.

## Unique mechanics
1. **Tunnel Network**
   - Tunnel Diggers build **Tunnel Entrances** (120 MP / 15 FU, camo 3) in owned or neutral territory. Every entrance joins the network graph.
   - **Travel:** a squad enters (3 s), travels at about 8 m/s along the graph, and exits at the chosen entrance. Each entrance handles 2 squads at a time. Squads underground are invisible and untargetable.
   - **Retreat:** squads may retreat to the nearest entrance instead of the HQ.
   - **Disruption:** if an entrance is destroyed while squads are moving to it, they reroute to the next nearest one.
   - **Ground cost:** rock ×2 cost and time; farmland ×1.2.
   - **Counters:** K9, drones, towers, D9R, IDF engineers' tunnel demolition, GBU-28, Intel Tunnel Reveal.
2. **Ambush**
   - Squads stationary in buildings or rubble gain camo 2.
   - Their first volley from camouflage gets +25% accuracy.
3. **Underground HQ.** The Command Bunker takes −60% damage from air and artillery. The GBU-28 deals full damage.
4. **Hidden production.** The Workshop, Arms Lab and Elite Command are camo 2 until spotted. They are revealed for 10 s each time they produce a unit.

## Veterancy flavor
- *Battle-Hardened:* +20% XP gain for all units.
- Retreating through a tunnel preserves XP even if the squad was nearly wiped.
- *Promotion:* Vet 3 Qassam Fighters can be promoted to Elite (60 MU), gaining camo 1 and +10% accuracy.

## AI play-style notes
Spreads tunnels early on sand maps, keeps squads stationary in rubble, times salvos while Iron Dome is reloading, mines likely tank routes with EFPs, and retreats through tunnels instead of to the HQ.
