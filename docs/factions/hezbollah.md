# Faction — Hezbollah: "Defense in Depth & Firepower"

## Identity
A semi-conventional force: ATGM masters, fortified hillsides, a deep rocket arsenal, drones and the only layered air defense in the game. It holds ground well, then breaks the enemy with firepower and Radwan assaults.

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Best AT infantry (Kornet, Almas) | Rocket Depots are high-value targets |
| Rocket Stockpile barrages | Limited armor (one T-72) |
| Strong fortifications, especially on rock | Fortifications are slow and costly |
| Layered AA denies IDF air power | Slower tech than Hamas |

**Visual identity:** uniform olive and woodland camo, more conventional gear, camouflage nets, hillside bunkers.
**Voice:** Lebanese Arabic. Disciplined, structured radio calls.

## Unique mechanics
1. **Rocket Stockpile (RS, cap 200)**
   - **Rocket Depots** (200 MP / 30 FU, max 3) generate +6 RS/min each. They are camo 2 while no enemy is within 30 m.
   - RS pays for Katyusha barrages, the Burkan and the Fadi. A destroyed depot loses 25 RS.
2. **Fortifications**
   - Bunkers and ATGM nests get **HP ×1.3 on rock** ground.
   - *Camouflage Net* (20 MU) gives +1 camo to any structure or emplaced weapon.
3. **Shoot-and-scoot.** After firing, MLRS trucks and launchers gain +50% speed for 8 s.
4. **AA umbrella.** MANPADS, ZU-23 and the "358" SAM form layered air denial.

## Veterancy flavor
- *Veteran Cadres:* any infantry squad can be bought at Vet 1 for +25% cost. This option is hidden when *Syria Veterans* is active, which already grants Vet 1 for free.
- Defensive kills (by units within 20 m of a friendly bunker) give +15% XP.

## AI play-style notes
Fortifies high ground and chokepoints, layers AA before the IDF reaches T4, hides depots, uses Double-Tap on Merkavas, and shoot-and-scoots MLRS between barrages.
