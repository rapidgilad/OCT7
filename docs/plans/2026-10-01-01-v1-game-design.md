## 1. Vision & Pillars
**Working title:** *Iron Swords: Frontlines* (placeholder; codename OCT7)
**Genre:** Squad-based tactical RTS (Company of Heroes 2/3 lineage), modern warfare, PC (Windows/Steam), mouse + keyboard.

| Pillar | What it means in play |
|---|---|
| **Asymmetry with parity** | Three factions play completely differently but have equal win rates. Precision and technology vs stealth and tunnels vs fortifications and firepower. |
| **Cover, terrain & destruction matter** | Urban rubble, hills, tunnels, and line of sight decide fights more than unit count does. |
| **Counter-play loops** | Every signature weapon has a counter: Iron Dome vs rockets, Trophy vs RPG/ATGM, drones/K9 vs stealth, MANPADS vs air power. |
| **Units are characters** | Veterancy, heroes, and native-language voices make every squad feel alive. |

**Match length:** 20–35 min. Early game (0–6 min): infantry and capturing. Mid game (6–15 min): support weapons and light vehicles. Late game (15+ min): armor, heroes at full level, special weapons.

### V1 scope
| IN | OUT (V2+) |
|---|---|
| 3 factions, all matchups including mirror | Multiplayer (1v1/2v2), replays for players |
| 1v1 skirmish vs AI — Easy / Normal / Hard | Story campaign and **cinematics** |
| 4 maps (+1 stretch) | Map editor / modding |
| Base building, tech tiers, defenses | Night maps, weather systems |
| 2 doctrines + 2 heroes per faction (pre-match loadout) | Additional factions |
| Veterancy (units Vet 0–3, heroes Lv 1–5) | Ranked play, cosmetics store |
| Special weapons per faction | |
| Full unit VO in native languages + English subtitles | |
| Short tutorial ("Basic Training") | |

### Content & platform rules (practical: store approval, ratings, regional law)
- **No civilian NPCs or civilian-harm mechanics in V1.** Maps are evacuated combat zones. No hostage mechanics. This keeps the ESRB M / PEGI 18 rating and Steam approval straightforward.
- **Heroes are fictional.** No real persons. Run a name check before lock.
- **Faction insignia and names are data-driven** (localization keys + swappable emblem textures). Germany bans Hamas and Hezbollah symbols, so a region-variant skin costs nothing if it's built in from day 1.
- **Original score only.** No real anthems or organization songs (copyright and platform risk).
- Religious sites and hospitals are not destructible or targetable map props.

## 2. Core Gameplay

### Economy
| Resource | Source | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Manpower (MP) | HQ base income + sectors | Infantry, buildings, reinforcements |
| Munitions (MU) | Munitions sectors | Upgrades, abilities, special weapons |
| Fuel (FU) | Fuel sectors | Vehicles, tech tiers, tunnels and fortifications |
| Command Points (CP) | Combat XP (all factions) | Unlocking doctrine abilities |
| **Faction-unique** | IDF: **Intel (INT)** · Hamas: **Tunnel Network** (mechanic, not currency) · Hezbollah: **Rocket Stockpile (RS)** | See factions |

Initial tuning:
- Start: 400 MP / 40 MU / 15 FU.
- HQ income per minute: 220 MP / 10 MU / 5 FU.
- Sector income per minute: Standard +15 MP; Munitions +25 MU; Fuel +15 FU. Building an outpost on a sector adds about 30%.
- Pop cap is 100 for everyone. Each pop point above 20 reduces MP income by 1.5/min (upkeep).
- IDF squads cost 7–8 pop and Hamas squads 4–6, so Hamas fields about 1.5× the unit count.

### Territory & win conditions
- The map is divided into sectors. Sectors must be connected to your HQ to produce income (cutting off supply lines matters).
- **Victory Points:** 3 per map, 500 tickets each side; controlling more VPs bleeds the enemy's tickets. Alternative modes: Annihilation (destroy all units and the HQ).

### Combat model (CoH-style, probabilistic)
- **Sim tick:** fixed 8–10 Hz, deterministic (see Engine section).
- **Hit chance** = Weapon accuracy(range) × Target received accuracy × Cover × Moving penalty × Veterancy.
- **Cover:**

  | Cover | Accuracy multiplier |
  |---|---|
  | Heavy | 0.5 |
  | Light | 0.75 |
  | Open | 1.0 |
  | Negative (crater/water/moving in the open) | 1.25 |
  | Garrison (building) | 0.6, worsens as the building takes damage |

  Rubble counts as heavy cover. **Hamas trait:** rubble 0.45 + camouflage while stationary.
- **Suppression:** each squad has a suppression meter → Suppressed (−50% speed, −accuracy) → Pinned (can only retreat). MGs suppress, snipers don't.
- **Retreat:** any squad can retreat (0.5 received accuracy, +speed) to a retreat point.
  - IDF: HQ, Field Aid Station, Namer APC
  - Hamas: **any tunnel entrance**
  - Hezbollah: HQ, bunkers
- **Reinforce:** at retreat points and in friendly territory near them.
- **Vehicles:**
  - Directional armor (front/side/rear). Penetration chance = penetration ÷ armor (clamped).
  - Critical hits on penetration: engine damaged (−50% speed), immobilized, main gun damaged, crew stunned, fire.
  - Engineers repair.
- **Interceptor system (one shared component):**

  | System | Mounted on | Intercepts | Notes |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Trophy APS | Merkava | RPGs/ATGMs, 85% chance | 3 charges; recharges 1 per 45 s out of combat; 40% vs top-attack; cannot stop IEDs/EFPs or large blasts |
  | Iron Dome | IDF structure | Rockets 90%, mortars 50% | 12 interceptors, MU to restock; **beaten by saturation** |
  | Iron Beam | Iron Dome upgrade | Drones and mortars | Laser, no ammo cost, slow cycle |

- **Detection / camouflage:**
  - Units have camo level 0–3 and detection level 0–3. A unit is revealed when an enemy with detection ≥ its camo is within detection radius.
  - IDF has the best detection (drones, K9, camera towers); Hamas and Hezbollah have the best camouflage.
- **Air call-ins** fly a real path and can be damaged or aborted by AA:
  - MANPADS and ZU-23 threaten Apache and drones.
  - Fast jets are only threatened by Hezbollah's late-game "358" loitering SAM.
- **Destruction:** buildings have damage states and collapse into rubble (heavy cover, partially blocks vehicles, updates the navmesh). D9 bulldozers clear rubble. Tunnels are unaffected except by bunker-busters.
- **Ground type** (per map zone): sand/urban vs rock. Rock doubles tunnel cost and build time and makes Hezbollah bunkers stronger.

## 3. Factions

### 3.1 IDF — "Precision & Combined Arms"
**Identity:** Expensive, elite, technological. The best armor, detection, and air support. **Weaknesses:** small army, every loss hurts, depends on Intel to use its precision weapons.

**Unique mechanics:**
- **Intel (INT, cap 100).**
  - Earned from drones/camera towers over time, first spotting of enemy structures (+5), kills (+3), and K9 finds (+2).
  - Spent to lock precision strikes and to reveal tunnel segments.
- **CASEVAC.** When a soldier falls, 50% chance of a "wounded" marker for 25 s. A medic or Namer can recover them, refunding 50% of the model cost with no XP loss.
- **Combined arms.** Tanks within 15 m of friendly infantry detect camo-1 AT teams.
- **"Tzeva Adom" alert:** 3–5 s audio/visual warning before incoming rockets.

**Tech tree:** Brigade Forward HQ → T1 Infantry Company Post (180 MP/15 FU) → T2 Combat Support Base (200/60) → T3 Armored Battalion Yard (300/120) → T4 Air Force Liaison Cell (250/150).

### 3.2 Hamas — "The Underground"
**Identity:** Cheap, numerous, stealthy. Tunnels, ambushes, IEDs, rockets, drones. **Weaknesses:** no armor, fragile in the open, low sustained firepower against tanks.

**Unique mechanics:**
- **Tunnel Network.**
  - Diggers build entrances (120 MP/15 FU, camo 3).
  - Squads travel the network graph: about 8 m/s + 3 s entry delay, 2 squads per entrance at a time. They are invisible and untargetable underground.
  - If an entrance is destroyed while squads are heading to it, they reroute.
  - Rocky ground doubles the cost.
  - Counters: K9, drones, D9, Bunker-Buster.
- **Ambush.** Squads in buildings or rubble that stay stationary get camo 2 and +25% first-volley accuracy.
- **Underground HQ.** The Command Bunker takes −60% damage from air/artillery, but the Bunker-Buster does full damage.
- **Hidden buildings.** Workshops are camouflaged until spotted.

**Tech tree:** Command Bunker → T1 Workshop (150 MP/10 FU) → T2 Arms Lab (200/40) → T3 Elite Command (250/80) → T4 Strategic Launch Site (300/120).

## 4. Veterancy & Heroes

### Unit veterancy (Vet 0–3)
- **XP sources:** damage dealt × target value, kill bonus, captures. Support units also earn XP from spotting, healing, and detection.
- **Thresholds** scale with unit cost class.

| Level | Infantry | Support weapons | Vehicles |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vet 1 | Unlocks unit ability, +10% accuracy | Faster setup | Unlocks ability, +rotation speed |
| Vet 2 | −10% received accuracy, +suppression resistance | +accuracy/range | +reload speed |
| Vet 3 | +15% accuracy, +20% reload, +10% speed | −received accuracy | +10% armor, self-repair to 50% out of combat |

- **Retention:** reinforcing keeps XP; a squad wipe loses it.
- **Faction flavor:**
  - IDF: CASEVAC keeps XP; "Hard Training" upgrade gives new squads +10% XP.
  - Hamas: +20% XP gain; tunnel retreat keeps XP; Vet 3 Fighters can be promoted to Elite for MU.
  - Hezbollah: veteran cadre purchases (start at Vet 1 for +25% cost).
- Chevrons on the HUD and voice lines change tone at higher vet.

### Heroes (choose 1 of 2 pre-match, unlocks after T1)
- Unique and one at a time. Level 1–5 with XP thresholds 0/400/1000/2000/3500.
- **Level unlocks:** Lv1 ability A → Lv2 aura (nearby units +10% accuracy / −10% received accuracy) → Lv3 ability B → Lv4 aura upgrade → Lv5 ultimate.
- **On death:** 120 s redeploy, keeps 50% XP, enemy gains +1 CP.
- About 150 unique voice lines each.

|---|---|---|
| IDF | **Maj. Ido "Ze'ev" Ronen** — Sayeret commander squad: target designation, breach & clear (flashbangs), night ops camo, **Ult: Deep Strike** (infiltrate behind lines) | **Lt. Col. Tamar "Lahav" Adler** — Merkava Mk.5 "Barak" commander tank: command aura (+reload), IronVision (reveals camo around the tank), extra Trophy charges, **Ult: Fire Weaver Network** (all artillery fires on her target) |

## 5. AI & Difficulty (biggest technical risk; starts in month 1)

**Architecture:**
1. **Strategic layer (Commander):** utility AI re-evaluated every 2–5 s.
   - Goals: economy, military, tech, defense, special weapons.
   - Build-order templates per faction × doctrine, with randomized branch points.
2. **Operational layer:** task forces get objectives from **influence maps** (threat, territory value, VP pressure, detected tunnels/launchers).
3. **Tactical layer:** behavior tree per squad — cover seeking, retreat thresholds, flanking, garrisoning, ability use, AT kiting, dodging artillery and grenades.
4. **Perception:** the AI obeys fog of war on all levels (no map hack). AI orders go through the same command queue as the player's.

| Parameter | Easy (Recruit) | Normal (Veteran) | Hard (Elite) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reaction delay | 2.5 s | 1.0 s | 0.3 s |
| APM cap | ~20 | ~50 | ~120 |
| Build order | Slow, generic | Faction-standard | Optimized, counter-builds vs scouted army |
| Cover / retreat | Poor cover, retreats late | Uses cover, retreats at 30% HP | Precise; preserves vet; reinforces smartly |
| Abilities used | ~40% | ~75% | ~100%, combos (smoke → assault) |
| Multi-prong attacks | No | Occasional | Yes, plus feints |
| Faction tricks | Basic | Tunnels/fortify | Tunnel ambushes, rocket saturation timed vs Iron Dome, Intel-driven strikes |
| Resource bonus | 0 | 0 | 0 (fair). "Insane +30%" is V1.1 |

**Tooling:**
- Headless AI-vs-AI batch runner (faster than real time) for balance and regression; thousands of matches per night.
- In-game debug overlay showing influence maps and goal scores.

  | Source | Lines |
  |---|---|
  | Each unit type | ~45 |
  | Each hero | ~150 |
  | Each HQ announcer | ~120 |

- **Pipeline:**
  - Script spreadsheet: ID, context, line, transliteration, English.
  - Placeholder TTS during development; studio recording at Beta (48 kHz/24-bit).

  - In-engine Timeline + Cinemachine for briefings and map intros.

## 9. Engine Recommendation & What We Need

### Recommendation: **Unity 6 LTS** (URP), C#
**Why:**
- Proven for this exact genre: **Iron Harvest** (CoH-style) and **Broken Arrow** (modern-warfare RTS) are both built on Unity.
- C# iteration speed and data-driven design via ScriptableObjects.
- DOTS/Jobs/Burst for hundreds of units, projectiles, and line-of-sight calculations.
- Strong RTS middleware ecosystem.
- Larger hiring pool.
- Free (Personal) under $200K revenue; Pro is a per-seat annual subscription.

**Alternatives:**

| Engine | Choose it if | Trade-offs |
|---|---|---|
| **Unreal Engine 5** | Top-tier visuals and cinematic marketing are priority #1 and the team has strong C++ | Lumen/Nanite are costly from a top-down camera with many units; Mass Entity is less mature for RTS; deterministic lockstep is harder; 5% royalty over $1M. Excellent for the future cinematics either way |
| **Godot 4** | Zero cost, open source, very small team | Weaker large-scale 3D performance and tooling, few RTS-grade plugins; higher risk |
| **Custom engine** | — | Not recommended for V1 |

**Hybrid option:** game in Unity, cinematics rendered in UE5 later. You get the best of both with no engine lock-in for cutscenes.

### Architecture (decide now, even though V1 is single-player)
- **Deterministic simulation core:** fixed 8–10 Hz tick, command-pattern input, fixed-point math for the sim. The presentation layer interpolates. This unlocks replays, the AI batch runner, and future lockstep multiplayer without a rewrite.
- **Core modules:**
  - Selection/commands
  - Pathfinding (A* Pathfinding Project Pro — recast navmesh, dynamic updates on destruction, RVO avoidance, vehicle turning radius)
  - Formations
  - Cover nodes
  - Combat resolver
  - Suppression
  - Vehicle armor/crits
  - Interceptor system
  - Line of sight / fog of war (height-aware grid + GPU fog texture)
  - Detection/camo
  - Territory/economy
  - Tech/construction
  - **Tunnel network graph**
  - Abilities framework (data-defined targeting, delay, effects)
  - Air call-ins with AA
  - Destruction states → rubble → navmesh update
  - Veterancy/heroes
  - AI
  - Bark manager
- **Data:** every stat in data. Balance spreadsheet → importer → ScriptableObjects/blob assets. Editor tooling via Odin Inspector.

## 10. Production Roadmap, Team, Budget, Risks

### Milestones (~22–24 months)
| Phase | Months | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-production | 0–3 | GDD locked; grey-box prototype: selection, move, cover, suppression, capture; 1 IDF + 1 Hamas squad; art style target |
| Vertical slice | 4–9 | IDF vs Hamas T1–T2, base building, vet, 1 hero each, 1 map, Normal AI, placeholder VO. **"Is it fun?" gate** |
| Alpha | 10–16 | Hezbollah; T3–T4; special weapons; doctrines; 4 maps; all 3 AI levels; VO casting |
| Beta | 17–21 | Content lock, VO recorded, AI-vs-AI balance, optimization, HE/AR localization, closed playtests |
| Release | 22–24 | Steam page, store review, rating, launch (Early Access optional at month ~14 with 2 factions) |

### Core team (~15)
| Role | Count |
|---|---|
| Game director/designer | 1 |
| Systems/balance designer | 1 |
| Lead programmer | 1 |
| Gameplay programmers | 3 |
| AI programmer | 1 |
| Tools/tech programmer | 1 |
| Technical artist | 1 |
| Environment artists | 2 |
| Unit/vehicle artist | 1–2 |
| Animator | 1 |
| VFX artist | 1 |
| UI/UX designer | 1 |
| Audio designer | 1 (VO outsourced) |
| QA | 1–2 |
| Producer | 1 |

**Budget bands (rough, loaded cost):**
- Lean: 6–8 people, heavy outsourcing and Asset Store use, about 30 months → **$0.8–1.5M**
- Standard: ~15 people, about 24 months → **$2–3.5M**

### Top risks → mitigation
1. **AI quality.** Start AI in month 1; build the headless batch runner early.
2. **Pathfinding with destruction and vehicles.** Prototype in month 1–2 with A* PP Pro.
3. **Three asymmetric factions = scope.** The vertical slice ships with 2 factions; Hezbollah joins at Alpha. Cut-line: 1 doctrine per faction.
4. **Store approval / regional law.** Data-driven names and emblems; content rules in section 1; early Steam review.
5. **Arabic VO casting.** Start at Alpha; use multiple vendors.
6. **Performance at 100 pop × 2 + destruction.** DOTS for projectiles and line of sight; set a perf budget from the vertical slice onward.
