# Plan — v0.1 "First Playable Skirmish" (1v1 vs AI, all 3 factions, rough low-poly graphics)

## Context
The M0 skeleton is on `claude/coh-rts-game-design-06vm8t`: a deterministic C# sim (tick, commands, A* pathing, movement, economy, data) plus a Godot 4.5.1 sandbox with box placeholders, 32 tests, MatchRunner, CI and a cloud hook.

The owner's direction:
- **Get to a real game first, balance later.**
- First target: **skirmish 1v1 vs AI with rough graphics (not boxes)**.
- **Cover both ways:** auto from building edges, and engineer-placed sandbags.
- **All three factions.**
- **Signature systems** (tunnels, Iron Dome, Trophy, Intel) **deferred** to the next milestone, along with veterancy, heroes and doctrines.

Constraint: only GitHub is reachable from the cloud; CC0 asset sites are blocked. So graphics are **procedural low-poly models built in code**, behind a model registry so `.glb` assets can replace them later.

Outcome: you launch the game and pick your faction, the AI's faction and difficulty. You play a full match on one map, against an AI that builds, captures, attacks and retreats, until victory or defeat by VP tickets or HQ destruction.

## Sim work (`sim/`, C# 9, deterministic, data-driven, tests for every rule)
1. **Data:**
   - New `game/data/weapons.json`: range, accuracy near/mid/far, damage, cooldown, penetration, suppression, setup time, AT/infantry flags.
   - New `game/data/structures.json`: tier, cost, build time, HP, footprint, produces, requires.
   - New `game/data/maps/ridge_outpost.json`: size, buildings (kind heavy/light), sectors (pos, type), HQ starts.
   - New `game/data/ai.json`: build orders per faction; Easy/Normal/Hard parameters.
   - Extend `UnitDef`: weaponId, sightRange, buildTime, structure requirement, reinforce cost.
   - Extend `GameDataValidator` for all of the above.
   - `MapFactory` loads maps from data. The sandbox stays for tests.
2. **Cover** (`World/CoverGrid`):
   - Auto: walkable cells next to a building get **heavy** cover; next to a low wall or fence, **light**.
   - Each cover cell stores a normal toward the obstacle. Cover applies only when the attacker is on the far side (dot product > 0.3).
   - Engineer **sandbags** are a structure that adds heavy cover cells; vehicles can drive through them.
   - Move orders snap to the best cover cell within 4 m of the click.
3. **Vision** (`World/Visibility`):
   - Per-player visibility grid with line of sight blocked by buildings, updated every 2 ticks.
   - Units can only target what their player sees. The AI reads only visible enemies. The renderer uses it for fog of war.
4. **Combat** (`Combat/`), following the docs/02 model:
   - Hit chance = accuracy(range band) × target cover × moving penalty × suppression.
   - Per-model HP; deaths reduce `Models`; a wiped squad is removed.
   - Vehicles: front/rear armor; penetration chance = pen/armor.
   - Auto-targeting: nearest visible enemy in range, with AT weapons preferring vehicles.
   - Explicit Attack command.
   - MGs and AT teams need setup time and fire only when stationary. Infantry fires on the move at 0.5×.
   - **Suppression:** suppressed ≥ 0.5 (−50% speed, −25% accuracy); pinned ≥ 0.9 (retreat only).
   - **Retreat (R):** to HQ, +30% speed, 0.5× received accuracy.
   - **Reinforce (T):** near HQ or a production building, costs MP per model over time. Healing near HQ.
   - The sim emits **events** (shot, hit, death, explosion, capture, built): output-only, so determinism is safe.
5. **Territory** (`Territory/`):
   - Sectors are a Voronoi partition of the capture points, with an adjacency graph.
   - Infantry capture: neutralize, then capture, with contested pause.
   - Income only from sectors connected to the HQ.
   - VP ticket drain.
   - Match end: tickets reach 0 or the HQ is destroyed.
6. **Production & construction** (`Production/`):
   - Structure entities with HP; HQ pre-placed.
   - **Build** command: an engineer walks to the site; cost is paid on placement; progress requires the engineer adjacent.
   - Per-structure queues (max 5) with costs, build time, pop cap and tech requirements. Rally points. Cancel refunds.
   - Structures can be destroyed.
7. **Commands:** Move (cover snap), Attack, Retreat, Reinforce, Stop, Produce, CancelProduce, Build, SetRally. All go through `CommandQueue`.
8. **AI** (`AI/SkirmishAi`): one implementation, with Easy/Normal/Hard as parameter sets.
   - Data build order, then weighted unit mix by tier.
   - Capper squads go to the nearest uncontested sectors.
   - Army groups attack enemy, VP or point sectors when strength exceeds a threshold, and defend sectors under attack.
   - Retreat below a health threshold; reinforce at base.
   - Sees only visible enemies.
   - Replaces `AdvanceAi`, which stays for the sandbox.
9. **StateHasher** covers all new state.

## Presentation work (`game/`)
- **ModelFactory + model registry:**
  - Each unit or structure maps to a procedural builder, or a `res://assets/*.glb` file if one exists.
  - Builders use a `MeshKit` helper on SurfaceTool for wedges, prisms and tapered boxes.
- **Infantry:**
  - Low-poly soldiers: helmet/head, torso + vest, arms, two legs, weapon.
  - Faction looks: IDF olive + helmet; Hamas mixed clothes + dark balaclava; Hezbollah woodland + cap. Team-color armband for readability.
  - Procedural animation: walk cycle, aim/fire recoil, crouch when suppressed, fall on death (corpse fades).
- **Vehicles:**
  - Merkava: sloped hull, rear-set turret, long gun, side skirts.
  - Namer: tall hull + RWS.
  - ZU-23 technical: pickup + twin gun.
- **Structures:** per-faction low-poly HQs and tier buildings (bunkers, containers, tents, camo nets), with a scaffold-rise effect during construction.
- **Map:**
  - Concrete blocks with window insets, rooftop water tanks and AC units; low walls; sandbag chains.
  - Ground with roads and noise; scattered olive trees, rocks and bushes (MultiMesh).
- **VFX:** tracers, muzzle flashes, dust hits, AT explosions with smoke, capture-flag color changes.
- **Overlays:**
  - Fog of war (ground overlay texture from visibility; enemy units hidden when not visible).
  - Territory tint and borders.
  - Health and suppression bars.
  - Cover preview shields at the cursor during a move.
  - Building ghost (green/red).
  - Range circles on selection.
- **UI:**
  - Top bar: resources + income, pop, both ticket counts, VP ownership.
  - Minimap: sectors, units, camera frame; click to jump.
  - Unit or structure card.
  - Command card with grid hotkeys, costs and tooltips: build menu for engineers, production for structures, R/T/S.
  - Main menu → skirmish setup (your faction, AI faction, difficulty) → match → Victory/Defeat screen ("Play again").
- **Audio:** deferred. No CC0 sources are reachable; add when sounds are supplied.

## Verification

  - Cover multipliers and direction; LOS and vision; hit chance; penetration and armor facing.
  - Suppression thresholds; retreat and reinforce; capture and connectivity; income and tickets.
  - Production, tech, pop cap; construction; sandbag cover; victory conditions.
  - AI legality (never spends resources it doesn't have).
- **MatchRunner, all 9 matchups × 3 difficulties:**
  - Deterministic.
  - Zero exceptions.
  - ≥ 90% of matches end in victory within 45 simulated minutes.
  - Hard beats Easy in > 70% of matches.
- **Godot:**
  - Headless import and full-match smoke test exit 0.
